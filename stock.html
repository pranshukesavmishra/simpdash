08 · Company overview Basic

What BPCL does

BPCL refines crude oil and sells the products across India, from petrol pumps and LPG cylinders to lubricants and aviation fuel.

A holding company headquartered in Mumbai. Its businesses span fuel services, Bharatgas, MAK Lubricants, refineries, gas, industrial & commercial, international trade and proficiency testing.
