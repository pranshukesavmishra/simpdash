Research notes

What the numbers say, in plain language.

✓Strengths

  • 01Cash conversion of 196% means reported profit is fully backed by operating cash.

  • 03Debt is moderate at 0.43× equity, and interest is covered 8.8 times.
  • 04₹17.50 per share paid in FY26 dividends, a 6.0% yield at today's price.

!Watch-outs

  • 01Profits swing with crude prices: net margin fell to 0.4% in FY23 before recovering.
  • 02RSI of 23.4 shows heavy recent selling; price is below both 50- and 200-day averages.
  • 03Foreign investors cut holdings by 5.25 pts and promoters by 1.96 pts over the year.
  • 04Current ratio of 0.79: short-term bills exceed short-term assets, normal for fuel retail.

The business

BPCL refines crude oil and sells the products across India, from petrol pumps and LPG cylinders to lubricants and aviation fuel.

A holding company headquartered in Mumbai. Its businesses span fuel services, Bharatgas, MAK Lubricants, refineries, gas, industrial & commercial, international trade and proficiency testing. Bharatgas provides end-to-end energy solutions for businesses.
